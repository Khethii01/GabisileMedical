Privacy Policy

What this website does and doesn't do with your information.

Last updated 24 November 2026

1. Who we are

Gabisile Medical Facility is a nurse-led private clinic at Suite 6, Keerath Centre, 2 Police Station Road, Isipingo. You can contact us at gabisilemedical@gmail.com or on 066 352 6383.

2. What this website collects

This website does not store, post or collect the details you type into the booking form. The form builds a WhatsApp message in your browser. When you press send, the message goes to us through WhatsApp.

3. Messages you send us

If you message or call us, we use your name, phone number and message only to reply, to book your visit and to provide care. Patient records are kept as required for healthcare record-keeping. Please don't send detailed medical information over WhatsApp. Discuss it with the nurse during your visit.

4. Third-party services

  • WhatsApp (Meta) carries your message and is governed by its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts loads the typefaces. Your browser requests them from Google, which may receive your IP address.
  • Google Maps is embedded on the Find us page and may set its own cookies when it loads.
  • Google reviews are shown on the home and Reviews pages. When the page loads, your browser asks our server for them, and our server gets them from Google. Reviewers' profile pictures load from Google's servers, which may receive your IP address.

We don't currently run advertising or analytics cookies on this website. If that changes, we will update this policy and ask for consent where required.

5. Your rights under POPIA

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) you may ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to its use. Email gabisilemedical@gmail.com and we will respond. You may also complain to the Information Regulator in South Africa.

6. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
