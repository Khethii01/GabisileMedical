FAQ

Clinic questions, straight answers.

What to bring, when we're open and how booking works.

Where is Gabisile Medical Facility?

We are at Suite 6, Keerath Centre, 2 Police Station Road, Isipingo, Durban. We are in the Isipingo Hospital building, facing the parking area, with a pharmacy next door.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Friday 09:00 to 16:00 and Saturday 09:00 to 13:00. We are closed on Sundays.

Do I need an appointment?

No. You can book on WhatsApp, call, or simply walk in. All three work. Booking on WhatsApp helps us have the nurse ready for you.

How do I book a visit?

Use the booking page to pick a service, day and time of day. It opens WhatsApp with your request already typed. You can also WhatsApp or call 066 352 6383 during clinic hours.

Who will I see at the clinic?

You see a private nurse practitioner with over 30 years of experience in healthcare, including local and international clinical work.

What should I bring with me?

Bring your clinic card and any medication you are taking. For baby wellness checks bring the Road to Health booklet. For a PDP medical bring your ID and the form.

Do you give vaccines for children?

Yes, childhood immunisations are given by a nurse and the record is updated on the spot. Message us first to check that the vaccine you need is in stock.

Can I get an IV drip without being checked first?

No. Every IV drip is given only after the nurse has checked you, including your blood pressure, medical history and allergies. If a drip is not right for you, she will tell you.

Can employers send staff to the clinic?

Yes. We do pre-employment medicals and routine medical surveillance at the clinic, and we can run wellness days at your premises with blood pressure and glucose checks done on site.

Is my information stored on the website?

No. The booking form only opens WhatsApp with a typed message. Nothing you enter is stored or posted anywhere by this website.

What if it is an emergency?

Call 10177 or go to your nearest emergency unit. We are a nurse-led clinic and do not replace emergency care.

Still not sure?

Send us a message and a real person will reply during clinic hours.
